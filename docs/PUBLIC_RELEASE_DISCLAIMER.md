# Public Release Disclaimer and Safe-Use Policy

**Status:** Pre-release policy draft.

Raw SQL Vault is a learning resource and collection of database engineering examples. It is not a turnkey product, managed service, certified system, or assurance that any example suits an actual organisation.

Materials are provided “as is” and “as available,” to the maximum extent permitted by applicable law. No guarantee is made that every script executes, every invariant is enforced across all write paths, every race condition is tested, any index is optimal, or any database will never crash, slow down, lose availability, or lose data.

Do not deploy examples directly to production without independent engineering and domain review. Confirm engine/version, extensions, permissions, data classification, threat model, migration plan, capacity, monitoring, backup/recovery, and acceptance criteria. Run exploratory or destructive tests only in isolated environments with disposable data.

This repository is not legal, medical, financial, accounting, tax, cybersecurity, safety, procurement, or regulatory advice. Institutional names and workflows are illustrative and do not imply official endorsement, affiliation, certification, legal compliance, or permission to integrate.

Fixtures must be synthetic unless a specific dataset has documented provenance, lawful reuse rights, and approval. Do not publish credentials, personal data, private operational records, or confidential institutional information. High-impact workflows need accountable human, privacy, security, fairness, and legal review.

Performance depends on engine/version, hardware, storage, network, schema, data distribution, concurrency, configuration, and workload. Backups are not proven until restore and application-level checks succeed. Define recovery objectives for each deployment.

Report vulnerabilities privately rather than exposing secrets or exploit details in public issues. See [Security Policy](../SECURITY.md).

The license decision remains open. Until an explicit license is committed, no open-source license or general redistribution permission is granted. Public visibility alone does not grant reuse rights; track [issue #72](https://github.com/PerezChris99/raw-sql-vault/issues/72).

Anyone adapting the material is responsible for validation, security, operation, monitoring, backups, recovery, legal authorization, and documenting limitations. This practical disclaimer is not jurisdiction-specific legal advice.
